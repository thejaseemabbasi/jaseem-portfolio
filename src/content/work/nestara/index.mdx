At CUBIX, I led product and project delivery for the [Southern California LMCC](https://www.socallmcc.org/lmcc-app) platform—helping modernize how field teams, administrators, and stakeholders coordinate labor-relations workflows across mobile and web.

## The challenge

Stakeholders lacked a unified view of operational progress, and field workflows relied on fragmented processes that slowed down adoption and day-to-day coordination.

- Operational KPIs were not surfaced in a single, actionable view for decision-makers.
- Product priorities needed tighter alignment between client stakeholders and engineering.
- User adoption depended on simplifying daily mobile workflows without losing reporting accuracy.

## What I did

I owned the product backlog, release roadmap, and sprint execution—working closely with client stakeholders to translate operational pain points into clear user stories, acceptance criteria, and a dedicated KPI dashboard.

By prioritizing high-impact workflow fixes first and validating each release with stakeholders, the team kept delivery focused on measurable adoption rather than feature bloat.

## Key decisions

**Put KPI visibility at the center.** Designing a clear KPI dashboard aligned leadership and operational teams around the same metrics, improving stakeholder alignment by 50%.

**Prioritize daily field workflows.** Streamlining the core mobile actions removed friction for everyday users and lifted platform adoption.

**Ship in focused increments.** Short feedback loops ensured each release solved a real operational bottleneck before moving to the next module.
