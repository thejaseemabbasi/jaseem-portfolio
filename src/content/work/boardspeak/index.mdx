At CUBIX, I partnered directly with the [WAGMI Defense](https://wagmigame.io/en) leadership team—including senior industry executives from Electronic Arts, Call of Duty, and Apple—to govern a **$1M+** mobile and Web3 game build from initial MVP through live store launch.

## The challenge

Building a competitive mobile game with blockchain and NFT integrations meant holding studio-grade quality standards across front-end gameplay, backend infrastructure, and smart-contract milestones.

- Coordinating 15+ specialists across mobile game engineering, backend, QA, and Web3 tracks.
- Meeting strict executive review standards from veteran game directors under tight release targets.
- Managing scope and velocity cleanly while moving from an initial MVP into live operations.

## What I did

I ran Agile sprint cadences across engineering and art/game tracks, aligned front-end, backend, and blockchain deliverables under clear milestone definitions, and maintained transparent executive reporting for client leadership.

As the MVP proved itself, I structured the commercial and delivery transition from a Fixed-Price SOW into an ongoing hourly Time & Materials (T&M) model to support continuous post-launch iteration.

## Key decisions

**Tighten sprint predictability first.** Breaking game features and Web3 dependencies into clear sprint commitments increased team velocity by 40%.

**Keep executive communication direct.** Structured sprint demos and early blocker escalation built strong client trust across complex technical decisions.

**Match the contract model to the product stage.** Transitioning from Fixed-Price MVP to T&M after core launch gave the client flexibility for live-ops updates without constant change-order friction.
