At OVRLOD, I led a 25+ member cross-functional team through a **$1M+** digital transformation for Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM)—migrating more than 5,000 pages across five core properties (`adgm.com`, `adgmacademy.com`, `alreemisland.ae`, and `disputeresolution.adgm.com`) onto Optimizely DXP.

## The challenge

Moving five high-visibility institutional sites simultaneously meant managing heavy content volume, strict regulatory expectations, and multiple vendor dependencies under a fixed-price statement of work.

- Over 5,000 pages had to be migrated, validated, and launched without disrupting live operations.
- Cross-team handoffs across 25+ specialists needed tighter sprint visibility and standardized tooling.
- Vendor and client SLAs required consistent tracking to keep defects and schedule risks contained.

## What I did

I governed the program from fixed-price execution through post-launch stabilization, managing timesheets, cost baselines, and sprint cadences while enforcing clear SLA thresholds across internal and vendor teams.

To improve execution consistency, I trained 20 team members on Azure DevOps workflows—standardizing backlog tracking, sprint reporting, and release handoffs across the program.

## Key decisions

**Standardize the workflow before scaling.** Rolling out structured Azure DevOps practices across 20 specialists eliminated ad-hoc status chasing and improved sprint predictability.

**Manage commercial health alongside technical delivery.** Tight tracking of timesheets, cost baselines, and change boundaries enabled a clean transition from the initial Fixed-Price SOW into an ongoing SLA-governed maintenance contract.

**Enforce SLAs early.** Tracking response and resolution targets weekly kept critical issues from compounding near release milestones.
